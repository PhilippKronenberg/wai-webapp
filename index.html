About the index

The WAI extracts a single dynamic factor from weekly, monthly and quarterly Swiss indicators. Missing and mixed-frequency observations are handled by data augmentation, the state equation carries stochastic volatility so crisis periods are not smoothed away, and the factor's loading on GDP is fixed at one — which is what makes the index interpretable as a GDP growth rate rather than an abstract index.

Shaded areas are 95% credible bands from the posterior. They are shown for the growth-rate views only: the level index is a cumulation of the growth rate, and the model does not supply a compounded credible interval for it, so no band is drawn rather than one that would look like a level band without being one.

Methodology and full empirical results: Kronenberg (2026), Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics, 162:10, doi:10.1186/s41937-026-00157-w, extending Eckert, Kronenberg, Mikosch & Neuwirth (2025), Journal of Applied Econometrics, 40(3), 270–290, doi:10.1002/jae.3104.

The index is produced by the open-source mfbdfm R package (documentation). This page reads a single CSV that the package writes; it performs no estimation of its own.
